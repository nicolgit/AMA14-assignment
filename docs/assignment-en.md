# European Aviation MRO Intelligence Platform

- Industry: Aerospace and aviation; headquarters in France
- Operating region: France, Germany, the United Kingdom, Spain, and the Netherlands
- Regulatory context: GDPR, EU AI Act, and sector-specific directives

## Business Challenge
A France-based maintenance, repair, and overhaul (MRO) provider servicing 340 aircraft for six European carriers faces:

- Unscheduled maintenance events causing an average of 11 hours of aircraft-on-ground (AOG) time per incident
- Parts inventory spread across 12 hangars with no real-time visibility, causing parts cannibalisation
- EASA documentation compliance requiring 4500 engineer-hours annually
- Engine health data from OEMs delivered in proprietary formats with no unified analytics layer
- Knowledge retention risk as 31% of senior engineers approach retirement age

## Transformation Objective
Build an AI-powered MRO intelligence platform that predicts maintenance requirements, optimises
parts supply, automates EASA documentation, and preserves engineering expertise.

## Azure Services
- Azure IoT Hub
- Azure Machine Learning
- Microsoft Fabric
- Azure Synapse Analytics
- Azure OpenAI
- Dynamics 365 Field Service
- Microsoft Purview
- Power BI
- Azure API Management

## Expected Outcomes
AOG time reduced from 11 hours to under 3 hours, parts availability at the point of need improved by 34%, EASA documentation effort reduced by 55%, and first-time-fix rate improved from 71% to 89%.

## AI Infusion Point
A remaining useful life model analyses engine telemetry across multiple OEM data formats. A parts demand forecasting AI optimises stock levels across all 12 hangars. A GenAI engineering assistant retrieves maintenance procedures and completes EASA task cards from structured voice input during maintenance operations.
