| Zone / access | HPC borescope ports 3–7 |

**Required tooling**
- Video borescope, 6 mm articulating probe
- Engine turning tool P/N GEN-TT-072

**Safety**
- WARNING: Engine cooled below 60 °C before probe insertion.
- CAUTION: Rotate spool in probe direction only.

**Work steps**

| Step | Action | Mech. | Insp. |
|------|--------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Confirm engine cooled < 60 °C | ☐ | ☐ |
| 2 | Open borescope access ports 3–7 | ☐ | ☐ |
| 3 | Insert probe at stage 4 inlet | ☐ | ☐ |
| 4 | Rotate N2; inspect each blade leading edge | ☐ | ☐ |
| 5 | Record findings (coating loss, nicks, cracks) | ☐ | ☐ |
| 6 | Close and torque access ports | ☐ | ☐ |

**Acceptance limits**
- Coating loss ≤ 15% airfoil per blade.
- Leading-edge nicks ≤ 0.8 mm → acceptable; > 0.8 mm → raise NRC, see TC-0002 path.
- Any crack → **reject**, raise NRC.
